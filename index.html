Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CineTheta completely free?
Yes! CineTheta is 100% free with no hidden charges, paywalls, or premium subscription tiers.
How do I receive app updates?
CineTheta has a built-in automated update checker in Settings ➔ Updates. You can also visit this official website to download new releases.
Why does the first video play sometimes take ~37s?
For NetMirror / OTT streams, CineTheta runs a silent background challenge bypass to generate an authenticated session token (t_hash_t). Once unlocked, this token is securely cached for 20 hours for instant playback.
Can I download movies to watch offline?
Yes! Just tap the Download button on any movie or episode to save it directly into your device storage.
